Modernizing Training Operations

The LGS-TMS is a state-of-the-art digital platform designed to streamline the entire training lifecycle for the Local Governance School. From trainee registration to digital certification, we ensure efficiency, transparency, and accessibility.

Centralized Database

Unified profiles for all government trainees.

Digital Certification

QR-verifiable certificates issued instantly.

Key System Features

Smart Registration

Official email verification flows and comprehensive profile management including posting history and qualifications.

RBAC Security

Granular control with roles for System Admins, Directors, Training Officers, Trainers, and Trainees.

Batch Scheduling

Intelligent batch management for Mandatory, Refresher, and Optional training programs.

Attendance Tracking

Multi-mode attendance marking via Manual input, QR Codes, or Biometric integration.

Assessments

Full evaluation suite including Pre-tests, Post-tests, Quizzes, and Assignments with grading.

Analytics & Reporting

Real-time dashboards providing insights into enrollment stats, pass rates, and training effectiveness.
